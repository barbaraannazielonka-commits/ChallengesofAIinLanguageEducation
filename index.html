Grounded in research

Insights into AI and Language Teaching and Learning
Edited by Yijen Wang, Antonie Alm & Gilbert Dizon
One of the books I'd recommend to any language educator navigating AI is Insights into AI and Language Teaching and Learning — and specifically Chapter 3, "Challenges of AI in Language Education," by Yuwei Wang and Benjamin Luke Moorhouse. That chapter, together with my own classroom practice, is what inspired me to build this website. The items marked core below come from their original framework; the items marked extended are additional challenges and the full solutions framework I developed from teaching.

About this resource

This guide was developed by Barbara Anna Zielonka, building on the research framework in Chapter 3 of Insights into AI and Language Teaching and Learning (Wang & Moorhouse, in Wang, Alm & Dizon, eds.). It combines that framework with additional challenges and a solutions framework developed through classroom practice. It's meant as a living reference — use what's useful, adapt the rest.
